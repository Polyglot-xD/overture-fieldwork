# Data attribution

© Overture Maps Foundation and its source contributors. Overture data is available
from [overturemaps.org](https://overturemaps.org/) under the applicable upstream
licenses preserved in each record and in [extraction-manifest.json](extraction-manifest.json).

This bundle selects and normalizes places from Overture release **2026-11-18.0**.
It retains GERS IDs, source datasets, upstream licenses, versions, coordinates,
and raw source properties. Human research fields have been removed from the
bundled snapshot. Normalization and selection do not verify current opening
status, independence, quality, or purchasing intent.

Applicable notice copies are included in `licenses/`: CDLA-Permissive-2.0,
Apache-2.0, CC0-1.0, and the full official Foursquare notice page. Foursquare
data was transformed to Overture’s schema and selected and normalized for this
product. See [Overture attribution](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/)
and the [official Foursquare notice](https://opensource.foursquare.com/places-notice-txt/).

`bundle-manifest.json` gives the bundled public file hashes.
